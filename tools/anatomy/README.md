Only iso-kit.mjs and the audit's three required modules are included. These are
build-time tools for ../build-drafting.mjs. They are not imported by the site
or shipped in its browser bundle. The generated artwork is checked in under
components/modern/drafting/art.json. The original MIT notice applies to these
files and the generated geometry derived from them.
